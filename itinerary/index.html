  • 📉Moving Tahoe to the 13th–15th cost one extra day: Saturday the 15th used to be a work day, and now it's the drive home.
  • 🌊Monterey moving to Sunday the 16th costs another: that Sunday used to be a work day, and it was buying back the flight home. Working Saturday the 22nd back in Texas recovers one — without it the total is 6 days / 48 hrs.

Don't forget

The things that will bite us if nobody handles them.

  • 🎟️Muir Woods needs a parking or shuttle reservation booked in advance at gomuirwoods.com — summer slots sell out, and there's no walk-up option.
  • 💺Ellie needs a booster seat. California requires one under age 8 or under 4'9". Still to decide: rent one with the van or fly hers out.
  • 🌊The tide is why Monterey sits on the 16th. Every really good low tide this trip lands before dawn. The 16th at 8:22pm (+1.4 ft) is the best one we can actually get to, and it's the only evening low that still has light on it — sunset is 7:58, full dark about 8:24.
  • 🥾Point Reyes lighthouse is a trap with little kids — about 45 minutes each way plus 300-odd stairs.
  • 🛣️The van covers 2,100 miles. Everything above adds up to roughly 1,575, so there's cushion — but Yosemite would eat it, which is part of why it's off the list.
